RZMT is a deeptech startup working at the intersection of software, connected hardware, automation and AI integration.

Technology grounded in practical needs.

Our purpose is to develop technology solutions that address practical industry challenges.

We begin with the problem and the people working through it. We examine the operating environment, identify what needs to improve and develop a solution that fits.

Our engineering principles

  • Build around a clear operational need.
  • Integrate existing technologies and standards where they fit.
  • Validate assumptions through prototypes and measurements.
  • Make claims that the evidence supports.

Flagship Platform

One industry.
A connected approach.

Our first flagship solution addresses Out-of-Home advertising.

Building OOH RAIL™

Shared Infrastructure. Accountable Execution.

RZMT™ is developing OOH RAIL™—shared, interoperable infrastructure connecting outdoor advertising assets, stakeholders and transactions.

Our new industrial solution, TAARAA OS™, is our unified operating platform for Static OOH, Transit OOH, DOOH and pDOOH within the Out-of-Home advertising industry. It runs on the OOH rail built by RZMT™.

The rail is designed to connect existing assets, software and service providers through a common evidence and workflow framework.
